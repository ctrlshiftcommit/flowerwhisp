# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the FlowerWhisp project welcoming and safe
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific, and curious. Assume good faith while taking reported
impact seriously. Welcome accessibility feedback, protect private data in bug
reports, and keep discussions focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
privacy violations, and disruptive personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainers using the security contact in
SECURITY.md. Do not include secrets, recordings, API keys, or personal data.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this code.
